# 8 — Testing & Iteration

**Ask what people know, not what they don't.**

Households didn't know their exact house area — but they knew how many floors they had, so we asked for that instead and estimated area from it. The same logic applied to electricity use: households didn't know their annual usage — asked directly, they guessed. We switched to asking household size instead, and estimated usage from that, with the option to enter an exact number manually. With fewer numbers to hunt for, completion time dropped by more than 10 minutes on average — fewer people stalled out, and more of them finished.

> "It was so much easier than expected."
> — Test user, Germany
