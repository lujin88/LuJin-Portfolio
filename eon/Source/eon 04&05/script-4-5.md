# 4 & 5 — The Journey & The Problem

**A narrow window, lost at Calculate.**

Visit → Explore → **Calculate** → Consider → Apply → Purchase

Homeowners typically made a decision within 24 hours of their first visit — but over 85% dropped off at Calculate before ever getting there. A slow, heavy calculator was costing us the entire decision window.

---

# 5 — The Problem

**The calculator asked for too much.**

A useful estimate required detailed information about the home. The original five or so questions were built for the algorithm, not for the customer. Some dropped off. Others never started.
