# Case: Swiss police investigation platform (AdNovum)

- Employer: AdNovum (Bern)
- Lu's role: designer (title: Senior Product Designer)
- Scope: a centralised investigation platform for the Swiss police, used across all 26 cantons

## Rollout and testing
- Started with the canton of Aargau; Zurich and Aargau were the main cantons.
- Tested in both the German-speaking and French-speaking regions. The Italian-speaking region was not part of testing.

## What Lu designed
Complex enterprise interfaces for expert users: data tables, filtering and bulk operations.

## Confidentiality
An internal police system: it is only used inside the police network and cannot be accessed remotely. Public-sector security work. Screens and internal details are not public; Lu can describe the process in an interview.
