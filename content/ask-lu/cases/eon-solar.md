# Case: E.ON Solar — landing page and solar calculator (at Futurice)

- Client: E.ON (Lu worked on it at Futurice)
- Lu's role: led the design end to end, with a junior designer and the wider product and dev team
- Duration: about six months, including implementation
- Result: solar lead conversion increased by 300% within two months of launch, from a ~5.1% baseline across Germany. A lead / conversion means a user submitted a quote request.
- The redesign was later adapted for the UK market

## Problem
People who were already interested in solar opened the calculator and were asked for roof size, roof angle, sun exposure and yearly electricity use. Most didn't know these numbers, planned to "do it later", and left. Only around 5% made it through.

## Research
Lu and the junior designer visited people in their homes across Germany. The team assumed sustainability and digital confidence would drive decisions; what people actually wanted was to understand things easily.

## What changed
- Progressive input: users answer step by step instead of facing every technical question at once
- A more transparent calculator, so users can see how their personalised offer is built

## How the result was measured
- Google Analytics, over the first two months after launch
- Hotjar heatmaps were used along the way to see where users clicked and scrolled, and to decide what to improve
