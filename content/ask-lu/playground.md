# Playground — personal projects

These are Lu's own side projects, built with AI tools (Claude, Cursor, v0). They are not client or employer work.

## Off We Go — AI family travel planner
- Input: destination, travel dates, budget, group size, preferred pace
- Output: a structured plan covering transport, accommodation, daily activities, car rental and estimated costs
- Idea: a personal workbench that combines several APIs (routes and maps, prices, opening hours) to automate travel-planning tasks Lu kept repeating
- Status: working prototype connected to live APIs; currently runs on Lu's own computer and is not publicly available
