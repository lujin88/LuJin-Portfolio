## AI in Lu's workflow
- In personal projects, Lu prototypes with Claude, Cursor and v0 to go from idea to working version quickly.
- At UBS, Lu also used AI in her design process, with Copilot. For compliance reasons, only bank-approved software could be used, which is why tools like Claude or Cursor were not used there. Client-identifying data (CID) is regulated and handled with extra care, so client data was not used freely with AI tools.
- Also at UBS, she worked with the design system team on AI components: proposing, reviewing and shaping them.
