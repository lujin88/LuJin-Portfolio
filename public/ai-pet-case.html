01The brief

Built for someone who can't read yet.

My kid wanted a pet to talk to. I wanted something that talks back kindly, teaches a little French, and never needs a grown-up to make it work. So nothing on the child's side could be text. Every state had to read through motion, sound and colour alone.

No words on screen

One dolphin, one big button. Everything else is animation.

One voice, always

The child only ever hears Diandian's voice. Never a robot.

Works anywhere

Outdoors on mobile data, with no laptop running at home.

Parents hold the keys

Setup, pairing and memory live behind a parent PIN.

02Meet Diandian

One character. Twelve feelings.

A five-year-old reads a face faster than any icon. Diandian has twelve short loops, and the app picks one for every moment of a call — listening, talking, nodding, comforting, even eating dinner together.

the trick →

Every loop starts from one 4×3 character sheet with an identity lock, so the horn, eyes and fur stay the same in all twelve clips. He always lies down — never stands — so every loop sits on the same baseline and the cuts between them disappear.

A phone is a grown-up's object. So Diandian also lives in a round screen inside a knob on the desk. Turn it to choose, press it to talk. No menus, no reading — the ring of light tells you where you are.

    05The voice

    He never sounds like a robot.

    A character dies the moment it switches voices. That set the whole architecture.

    HearChild speaksSpeech recognition on the device. A short pause means "my turn is over".
    ThinkDoubao chatA character prompt with Diandian's memory and the day's French word.
    SpeakPicture-book voiceDoubao's children's storybook voice — the only voice the child hears.
    ShowTwelve loopsThe matching motion plays while he talks, listens or waits.

    The decision: silence over a wrong voice.

    The easy fallback was the phone's built-in speech engine. It would have made every failure "work". I removed it: if the voice service fails, Diandian waits and tries again instead of suddenly sounding like a sat-nav.

    The one exception is deliberate — French words use a native French voice, because that is what a teacher would do.

    06Trust

    Fun for the child. Boring for an attacker.

    A toy that talks to a child is a device in a child's bedroom. I designed the parent side as carefully as the dolphin.

    Parent PIN

    6–8 digits, hashed. Wrong guesses lock it for longer each time.

    One-time pairing

    A new device pairs with a code that works once and expires in 10 minutes.

    Keys stay home

    AI service keys never leave the server. Devices get their own limited pass, revocable any time.

    No recordings

    The phone never uploads the child's voice. The knob sends speech only to recognition, and nothing is stored.

    Safe replies

    Every answer passes a filter. Anything unsafe becomes a gentle change of subject.

    Diandian's memory

    Parents can see what he remembers about the child, on their own page.
